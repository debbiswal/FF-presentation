*will I survive in a real project?*
*(straighten posture, smile slightly)* But desperation gave me courage.

### **Slide 5 – Bigger Fears**

Fear shows up daily. Code failing on my machine… deployments crashing… outages hitting thousands of customers.
*(look straight, strong voice)* At Airbus, fear takes bigger forms—safety risks, delivery delays, reputation on the line.

*Voice:* Serious, firm. Slow down on “safety” and “reputation.”

### **Slide 6 – Shrinking Fear**

But fear shrinks when faced together. We are never truly alone.
*(smile, gentle nod)* Teams turn fear into strength, and every failure into learning.

*Voice:* Uplifting, reassuring.
*Body:* Step closer to audience, open arms.

### **Slide 7 – The Message**

Failures teach us more than success. They stay, they shape, they prepare us.
Failure isn’t the opposite of success—it is part of it.
*(pause, firm voice)* Every fear faced, every failure endured… is a stepping stone to growth.

*Voice:* Strong, inspiring. Pause before the last line.
*Body:* Stand tall, make eye contact across the room, right hand slightly raised.
