
**Slide 1:**
I’m an Electronics graduate. After a short job search, I cracked my first opportunity in the computer field. With problem-solving and communication as my strengths, I took a bold decision—stepping into the software industry.

---

**Slide 2:**
Once inside the company, I went through classroom training. Topics came rushing—databases, UI, deployment, Git, and more. I couldn’t grasp much, and fear crept in. *Will I be able to sustain in real projects?*
But desperation fuels creativity. I became Batman—attending classroom training by day, and online classes by night.

---

**Slide 3:**
Soon, I began talking to my peers. To my relief, I wasn’t alone—many were facing the same struggles. Together, we pushed through, and finally, the training phase came to an end.

---

**Slide 4:**
My first team welcomed me warmly. Everyone was supportive, though challenges popped up along the way. Some days were tough, others full of fun and team parties. My project was built with .NET, Node.js, and JavaScript—a true rollercoaster ride of learning.

---

**Slide 5:**
Fears take many forms in our professional journey.
From “It works on my machine” moments… to frustrating deployment failures… to service outages impacting thousands of customers.
In Airbus, precision is non-negotiable—fears can mean customer safety, timely delivery, and even reputation at stake.

---

**Slide 6:**
But here’s the truth—*we are not alone*. When we stand together as a team, fears shrink. Collaboration turns challenges into learning opportunities.

---

**Slide 7:**
Failures teach us more than success ever can. They stay with us, shaping us.
Failure isn’t the opposite of success—it is part of the journey. Each setback becomes a stepping stone forward.
